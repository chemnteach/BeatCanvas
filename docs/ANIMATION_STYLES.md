## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Show Examples**: Keep 1-2 sample videos for each style to show potential clients
2. **Offer Style Tests**: Let artists preview 1-2 frames in their chosen style before full video
3. **Genre Matching**: Suggest styles based on artist's genre, but let them choose
4. **Bundle Deals**: Offer multi-video packages for artists releasing albums
5. **Style Mixing**: Advanced option - combine 2 styles (e.g., Watercolor + Ghibli)
